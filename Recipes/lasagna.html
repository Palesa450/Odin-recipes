Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, cheese and tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Minced beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 degrees celsius
  2. Cook the minced beef with onion and garlic
  3. Add the tomato sauce and simmer
  4. Layer the lasagna sheets with the meat sauce and cheese
  5. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used
  6. Bake for 30-40 minutes until golden
  7. Allow to cool slightly before serving
